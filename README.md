# music-future-with-you

> 日本語のREADMEはこちらです: [README.ja.md](README.ja.md)

A song and musical score for the opening of the Hokuriku Shinkansen.

## Features

- FLAC and MP3 audio files
- MIDI and PDF sheet music
- TXT and PDF lyrics

## Data

The audio, music, and lyrics files are provided by Fukui Prefecture for the purpose of promoting the opening of the Hokuriku Shinkansen.

## License

The copyright and all other rights to the audio and lyrics belong to Fukui Prefecture. Use of the song, including reproduction and distribution to third parties, is freely permitted for the purpose of generating momentum for the opening of the Hokuriku Shinkansen, as long as it does not "damage the dignity of Fukui Prefecture" or "pose a risk of violating laws or public order and morals".
